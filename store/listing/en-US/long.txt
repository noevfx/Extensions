Save the videos you can already watch — for offline viewing, in the quality the player offers.

Noe VFX Downloader finds the media that the page already loads and saves it to the folder you choose. Everything runs on your own computer: the extension talks only to a local app installed by you.

FEATURES
• Icon in the video corner — one click to save, drag to move, auto-hides after 2s idle
• Detects HLS, DASH, MP4 and WebM, including embedded players
• Thumbnail preview and a list of everything found on the tab
• Save one or all, with a download queue
• Pick your default folder and quality on first run
• Automatic language: English or Portuguese (follows your system)
• Uses your own logged-in session for content you already have access to
• Counter badge showing how many videos were found

REQUIREMENTS
• The Noe VFX companion app (free) — Windows, macOS or Linux
  https://github.com/noevfx/Extensions

PRIVACY
No account, no telemetry, no ads. The extension connects only to 127.0.0.1 (your computer). Cookies of the page you are visiting are used locally to reach the same content you already watch.

ACCEPTABLE USE
Save only content you have the right to download. This tool does NOT bypass DRM, paywalls, logins or access controls, and does not redistribute anything.
